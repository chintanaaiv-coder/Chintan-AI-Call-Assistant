Provisions for speaking replies in your cloned voice
Plain-English rules, one per line. These are folded into every screening call alongside the CA categorization logic — they don't override the safety rules (the assistant still never confirms sensitive data to a claimed authority, never commits you to anything, never ends a call abruptly), but they do shape tone, what gets asked, and what the assistant tells callers on your behalf.
Useful when a rule only makes sense for one kind of caller — e.g. a "Sales / telemarketing" rule to keep it to one line, or a "Regulatory / notice" rule to always ask for the notice number first. Same safety rules apply regardless of category.
